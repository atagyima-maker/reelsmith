    01 Describe your videoName your video

    This is the standalone studio: edit, voice, score and render here. Script writing, AI images and prompt packs run in the Claude version of Reelsmith. Write there, export the project file from My projects, then import it here. You can also add scenes by hand below.

    Scripts are written by Claude, with an invite code or your own Anthropic API key. Your idea is sent to Anthropic to write it; nothing else leaves this browser.

    Unlock AI writing

    AI writing needs an invite code or your own Anthropic API key.

    Your key goes straight from this browser to Anthropic, and Reelsmith's server never sees it. Each script costs a few cents on your Anthropic account. Use a separate key with a spend limit (console.anthropic.com), and only on a device you trust.

    02 Scenes

    Edit captions and voiceover lines, set timing, and drop your own photo or clip on any scene. Tap a thumbnail to jump there.

    03 Footage and sound

    Add photos or clips, then fill scenes in order. Each scene's Shot line tells you what footage fits.

    Add photos / clips

    AI images Free · Claude

    Claude draws an illustration for each scene from its Shot line and colours. In the video, each one moves with a slow camera push, so it plays like footage.

    Prompt pack Midjourney · Runway · InVideo

    For photoreal shots, Claude writes three prompts for every scene, matched to your look: a still image for Midjourney, a motion prompt to animate it in Runway, and a text-to-video prompt for InVideo. Generate only the shots you need, then drop the clips back onto your scenes.

    Music Free · composed for your cut

    Pick a mood and Reelsmith writes an original score that fits your video's length, builds to the last scene and lands accents on your cuts.

    Sound effects

    Whooshes on cuts, plus a hit, glitch or typewriter clicks that match each title card's animation. They follow your edits automatically.

    AI voiceover Free · Kokoro

    Voices are made on this device by the open-source Kokoro model, free and with no limits. The first use downloads the voice engine (about 90 MB); after that it loads from this browser's cache. Each scene's voiceover line becomes a voice clip, and the scene length follows it.

    1. Pick a voice and press Copy voice request.
    2. Paste it to Claude in chat. You get one MP3 per scene, named 01.mp3, 02.mp3…
    3. Add all the clips here at once. Each scene gets its clip, and scene lengths snap to the voice.

    04 Render

    Fast render builds the MP4 frame by frame, usually several times quicker than real time. Keep this tab open while it runs.

    How did your video turn out?

    Reelsmith counts anonymous usage, like how many videos get rendered, to decide what to improve. No cookies, nothing that identifies you, and nothing if your browser asks not to be tracked.
